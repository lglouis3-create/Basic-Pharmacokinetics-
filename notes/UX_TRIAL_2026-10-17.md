# PHAR 4221 drill: journeys 1 and 3 on an older build

Page rendered: `scratchpad/r5verify/PHAR4221_Drill.html` (older build), Chromium through Playwright, light theme (System), viewports 390×844 and 1280×900. Each run started with empty storage. Screenshots: `scratchpad/r5verify/shots/`. Step logs (JSON, one record per step, with each tile and box's `disabled`, class, computed opacity, pointer-events and size): `scratchpad/r5work/j1_*.json`, `j1t_*.json`, `j3_*.json`. ## Short assessment

Journey 3 (problem sets) works from start to finish at both widths. Parts are numbered "part 3 of 7 · 4 to go", the unit sits beside the number box, a unit typed after the number is accepted, and a part met alone in a practice quiz says which set it belongs to. Journey 1 has one blocking defect. In Build it, every equation after the first opens with its correct pieces greyed out and disabled. Check stays disabled, so the only way forward is Show me, and Show me records a miss. Type it works. Plan the problem works, but in about a third of the set-up explanations the "Why this line" text shows raw `{{frac:` markup. One problem set (cefprozil, ws4-p3) has parts that cannot be answered when they are met alone.

## Strengths to keep

- No step scrolled the page sideways at either width (`scrollWidth == clientWidth` was logged at every step).
- Build it: a tapped box shows as selected (`eqslot sel`, `aria-pressed=true`) and the hint changes to "Box selected: tap a piece to put it there…". Tapping a filled box empties it and gives its piece back. Stacked lines (C<sub>avg</sub><sup>∞</sup>, F<sub>rel</sub>) are drawn as a numerator over a bar over a denominator (`j1_390_b2_a_loaded.png`).
- Type it: capitals and missing spaces are accepted (`CMAX^(∞)*E^(-KΤ)` was marked correct), Enter submits, and the field gets focus on each new equation. "No, I was right" turns the verdict into "Counted as right" and updates the tally.
- Plan the problem: each step locks once it is answered (its buttons go to `disabled`, and the unchosen ones drop to opacity 0.55). Each step gets its own verdict ("✗ Not ClT: the stem asks for AUC"). The givens step marks "not needed" values.
- Problem sets: ws6-b2 restates the earlier values in every part, e.g. "(The regimen from part a: 138.16 mg every 8 hours to an 82-kg patient.)". A wrong entry is answered with "the answer is 1.576 mg/L (within ±0.032 mg/L counts), and you entered 2.364 mg/L", followed by the miss-kind chips. When a part is met alone, the page says "She sets this as part 3 of 7 of one problem … Work the whole set from the start".

## Highest-impact issues

### 1. Build it: after Next, the right pieces are disabled before anything is placed (observed, P1)

1. **Where:** Equations tab, Build them from pieces, piece tray. Built page function `eqLoad` (source counterpart `src/views.js`, `eqLoad`).
2. **What works:** the first equation of every run builds and checks correctly at both widths. Clear resets the tray.
3. **Observed:** `eqLoad` in the built page does not reset `EQ.trayOf` or `EQ.sel`. When the next equation opens, any tile whose index matches a piece placed in the previous equation is rendered `class="eqtile used"`, `disabled`, opacity 0.32, with every box still empty. Results:
   - 390 px: equations 2–5 (cavg-ss, cp-n, thalf-abs, cmax-ss-oral) had 2, 4, 1 and 4 of their correct pieces disabled. Check stayed disabled and the run ended "1 right of 1" after five equations (`j1_390_b2_a_loaded.png`, where both F D<sub>0</sub> and Cl<sub>T</sub>τ are greyed while the boxes are empty).
   - 1280 px: equations 2–5 were blocked the same way (`j1_1280_b2_a_loaded.png` … `b5`).
   - Equation 3 at 390 opened with box 3 already selected, carried over from equation 2 (`eqslot sel` before any tap).
   - In the 1280 run that recovered with Clear, cmin-ss opened with the correct "(1 − e<sup>−kτ</sup>)" disabled and the lure "(1 + e<sup>−kτ</sup>)" enabled. The only tappable piece of that shape was the wrong one.
4. **Why it matters:** a student who knows the equation cannot place it. Her ways out are Show me, which calls `eqRecord(…, false)` and resets the "learned" streak, or picking a lure. Five equations in a row become four recorded misses. Pressing Clear before placing anything restores the tray, and with Clear all five were correct (`j1_1280_clear.json`, "5 right of 5"). Nothing on the screen points to Clear.
5. **Change:** have `eqLoad` reset `EQ.trayOf = {}` and `EQ.sel = null`. The current `src/views.js` already does this, and `src/equation_test.py` (lines 114–120) checks for it. The deployed page needs a rebuild from current source. Confirm the deployed file is not this build.
6. **Alternatives:** none needed. A tray-state reset is the whole fix.
7. **Priority:** P1. Effort: a rebuild. Dependency: the deployed file must come from the current source.
8. **Verify:** run the existing `equation_test.py` check against the built `PHAR4221_Drill.html`, not only against the source. Also add this check: after Next, `document.querySelectorAll('.eqtile[disabled]').length === 0` and `.eqslot.sel` count is 0, for five equations in a row.

### 2. Set-up and Plan the problem: "Why this line" cuts fractions apart at the decimal point (observed, P2)

1. **Where:** Equations tab, Plan the problem (and Set-up only), answered card, "Why this line" list. `suQuestionHTML`, the line `const why = (String(q.setup.why).match(/[^.?!]+(?:[.?!]+|$)/g) …` (the same line is in `src/views.js` 4454).
2. **What works:** fractions without a decimal render stacked in the same list (AUC<sub>IV</sub> = D<sub>IV</sub> over Cl).
3. **Observed:** the sentence splitter breaks on the "." inside `{{frac:0.693|t½}}`. Stem ws7-3b shows "Hinges: k = {{frac:0." as one bullet and "693|t½}}, then Cl<sub>T</sub> = k × V<sub>D</sub>…" as the next (`shots/j1p_390_p2_crop_why2.png`). The same splitter run over the bank breaks a `{{…}}` span in **104 of 327** set-up explanations, among them m1-ord-n03, m2-n-t1, m2-n-999a, inf-n1 and ws4-3f.
4. **Why it matters:** k = 0.693/t½ is the most-used hinge in the course. Raw markup across two bullets hides the relation the drill is teaching.
5. **Change:** take out `{{…}}` spans before splitting and put them back after, or split only on `[.?!]` followed by whitespace and a capital letter or the end of the text.
6. **Alternatives:** render `why` as one paragraph without splitting. That loses the bullet scan.
7. **Priority:** P2. Effort: small, one function. No dependencies.
8. **Verify:** in `equation_test.py`, for every question with `setup.why`, render the card in plan mode with the line answered and assert that the `.why` text contains no `{{` or `|`.

### 3. Cefprozil problem set: parts f, g and h cannot be answered when met alone (observed, P2)

1. **Where:** Topics → Module 4 → Calculations → Single problems (types m4-cl and m4-crcl), and any practice quiz that draws ws4-3f, ws4-3g or ws4-3h. Bank file `src/q9_worksheets.js`, chain `ws4-p3`.
2. **What works:** worked through in order (Topics → In parts), all eight parts make sense, because part e's stem holds the change.
3. **Observed:** only part e says "his serum creatinine sky-rocketed to 2.8 mg/dL". Parts f ("Provide a new estimate of the total body clearance … assuming his volume of distribution is unchanged"), g and h repeat the original stem, with SCr 0.82 mg/dL, and give no new value. Their keys (37.29, 22.38 and 14.91 mL/min) depend on part e's CrCl of 35.49 mL/min. Met alone, f was question 17 of 35 at 390 px and question 5 of 35 at 1280 px (`shots/j3_1280_ws4-p3_single_ws4-3f_a.png`). The line "She sets this as part 6 of 8" appears only after the answer is checked.
4. **Why it matters:** a student working from the stem gets 149 mL/min (the part b value) and is marked wrong on a correct method. Weak spots then records a clearance miss that did not happen.
5. **Change:** append the bracket in her form: f "(Part e: SCr is now 2.8 mg/dL; CrCl = 35.49 mL/min.)", g "(Part f: Cl<sub>T</sub> = 37.29 mL/min.)", h "(Parts f and g: Cl<sub>T</sub> = 37.29 mL/min, Cl<sub>R</sub> = 22.38 mL/min.)".
6. **Alternatives:** show the "part X of Y" line above the stem before answering, for parts after the first. This tells her a value is missing but does not supply it.
7. **Priority:** P2. Effort: three stem edits. No dependencies.
8. **Verify:** add to `problemset_test.py`: for each chain part after the first, a number that its own `steps` take from an earlier part's `answer` must also appear in its stem. This heuristic flags 64 parts in the built page (`scratchpad/r5work/chain_carry_candidates.txt`). Most can be recomputed from the stem data (for example ws4-3c uses part b's 149 mL/min, which the stem's data reproduce), so treat the list as a review queue. ws4-3f, g and h are the confirmed cases.

### 4. Plan the problem: the session tally counts only the line step (observed, P3)

1. **Where:** Plan the problem, session row ("N of N right"). `suHTML` and `suPick`.
2. **What works:** each step's verdict is correct, and the end page reports "plans complete" separately.
3. **Observed:** after plan 2, whose first step was wrong ("✗ Not ClT: the stem asks for AUC" at 390 for ws7-3b; "✗ Not AUC" at 1280 for m4-n-ab2), the row read "2 of 2 right" (`j1t_390.json`, `j1t_1280.json`).
4. **Why it matters:** the visible score credits a plan whose first decision was wrong, and the drill exists to train exactly that decision.
5. **Change:** label it "lines right", or show "plans complete" next to it while in plan mode.
6. **Priority:** P3. Effort: small.
7. **Verify:** in plan mode, pick a wrong asked symbol, then the right line. The row text must not read as a fully right plan.

### 5. Touch targets under 44 px on the phone (observed, P3)

The Build it boxes are 52×40 px (`eqslot`). The Plan step buttons (asked symbol, unit, model, printed or not, givens) are 37–39 px high at 390 px (`j1t_390.json`, `h` field). The tiles (44 px) and the Check and Next buttons (43–45 px) are close to the target. Change: set `min-height: 44px` on `.eqslot` and on the plan option buttons in `src/shell.html`. Verify: a sweep check that every visible button in `#v-eq` measures at least 44 px high at 390 px.

### Present but within criterion: the carried-value format

ws6-b2 carries earlier values as "(The regimen from part a: …)" or "(The dose is the 550 mg recommended in part a.)" (ws6-b4), not "(Part a: …)". Only 5 stems in the whole build use the exact "(Part x:" form. A student gets what she needs either way, so no change is needed for understanding. A test written for the exact bracket form would flag these stems.
